🥤 Drinks & snacks between meals

Water all day. Black coffee or green/herbal tea (no sugar) mid-morning. If hungry between meals: a few almonds, cucumber, or bell-pepper sticks.

🫘 Beans & lentils

This plan uses canned beans & lentils — just rinse before use. If you buy dried beans, soak them in water overnight the day before, then drain and throw away the soaking water before cooking. (Lentils need no soaking — just rinse.)

Make it stick

Tips for reversing insulin resistance

Diet does the heavy lifting, but these simple habits help your body respond to insulin better.

🍞 Cut the sugar & white carbs

The biggest lever. No soda, juice, candy, white bread, white rice or pastries. The meals here lean on protein, vegetables, beans and healthy fats instead.

🥗 Fill half the plate with veggies

Non-starchy vegetables add fiber that slows sugar absorption. Greens, broccoli, peppers, zucchini, green beans and cauliflower all count.

🚶 Walk after meals

A 10–15 minute walk after eating helps muscles pull sugar out of your blood. Easy and surprisingly effective.

💧 Drink water first

Thirst can feel like hunger. Have a glass of water before snacking, and keep water with you through the day.

😴 Protect your sleep

Short sleep worsens insulin resistance. Aim for 7–8 hours; keep a steady bedtime.

⏱️ Keep meal times steady

Eating at the same four times daily steadies your blood sugar and curbs grazing. Try to finish dinner a few hours before bed.

One more time: This plan is educational only and not a substitute for care from your doctor or a registered dietitian. If you feel shaky, dizzy, or unwell after changing how you eat, check in with a medical professional.
